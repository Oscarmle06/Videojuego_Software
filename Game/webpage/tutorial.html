Everything you need to know to dominate the track

Steering speed scales with your current velocity — the faster you go, the sharper you can turn. Driving on grass significantly reduces your speed, so stay on the track.

Race Mechanics
HP — Hit Points

Your kart starts with 50 HP. Every collision with another kart costs life. Drop to 0 HP and you lose the race. Use recovery cards to stay alive.

Laps and Checkpoints

You must pass every checkpoint in order for the lap to count. The lap counter and minimap show your progress in real time.

Card Selection Between Laps

At the end of each lap a card selection screen opens. Choose between passive cards (permanent stat upgrades) and active cards (one-use abilities stored in up to 3 slots, triggered mid-race with O + P).

Race Position

The HUD shows your current position against the CPU rivals. Finish in 1st place after all laps to win.

Passive Cards — Permanent Upgrades

Passive cards apply stat upgrades instantly and remain active for the whole race. Picking the same card twice levels it up and doubles the effect.

Active Cards — In-Race Abilities

You can hold up to 3 active cards at once. Use O to cycle through slots and P to activate the selected card. Cards are consumed on use.

Tire Shredder
Active
Reduces the speed of the nearest rival ahead of you.
Grappler Hook
Active

Active

AI Rival Personalities
Fast

Speed above all else. Always on full throttle, takes corners at maximum velocity. Dangerous on straights, more manageable through tight curves.

Aggressive

Deliberately aims to crash into you. Uses active cards offensively and will sacrifice speed for a clean hit. Stay out of its range.

Evasive

Avoids collisions and uses defensive cards strategically. Hard to hit with your active cards. Competes through pure speed rather than contact.

Strategic

Calculates every move. Saves cards for the exact right moment, manages HP carefully, and decides when to attack versus when to hold back. The hardest rival.

Tips
Combine passive and active cards. Aerodynamic Spoiler + Grappler Hook is a strong combo — you go faster and also steal speed from rivals.
Stay on the track at all costs. Grass drops your speed significantly and can cost you multiple positions in seconds.
Do not ignore your HP. Using a Repair Bot at 30% health is far better than waiting until the last moment. Temporary Armor is ideal before a straight where you expect collisions.
EMP and Sonic Wave affect multiple rivals simultaneously. Use them when surrounded to create space fast.
You have 3 active card slots. Use O to reorder priority and always have the right card ready for the situation.
