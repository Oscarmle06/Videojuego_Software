HOW TO PLAY
W Accelerate
S Brake / Reverse
A Steer left
D Steer right
O Cycle active card slot
P Use selected active card

Steering scales with speed — the faster you go, the sharper your turns. Driving on grass reduces speed significantly.

Passive cards apply permanent stat upgrades. Selecting the same card twice levels it up.

Aerodynamic Spoiler
Passive
+Top speed · +Acceleration
Heavy Chassis
Passive
+20% max HP. Restores HP when applied.
Sport Tires
Passive
+Steering speed for tighter corners.
Racing Transmission
Passive
Reach top speed much faster.

Hold up to 3 active cards. Press O to cycle slots, P to activate. Cards are consumed on use.
