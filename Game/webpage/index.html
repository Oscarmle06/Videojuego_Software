What is Velocity Draft?

A mix of arcade racing, roguelike, and card strategy

2.5D Racing

Immersive Mode-7 style perspective with real-time rendering. Dynamic sky (dawn, day, dusk), roadside trees, and high-octane visual effects.

Card System

Between laps, choose passive upgrades that boost your stats or active cards you deploy mid-race: Spoiler, Grappler Hook, Sonic Wave, EMP, and more.

AI Personalities

Four rivals with their own AI: the Fast pushes flat-out, the Aggressive crashes into you on purpose, the Evasive dodges everything, and the Strategic calculates every move.

Procedural Tracks

Each race generates a unique circuit using Catmull-Rom curves. No matter how many times you play, you will never run the same track twice.

HP Management

Every collision costs HP. Hit zero and you lose the race. Use Repair Bots, Temporary Armor, and Pit Stops to stay on track.

Roguelike Mechanics

Every run is fresh. Card selection forces strategic decisions that shape how you face each lap — no two races play alike.
