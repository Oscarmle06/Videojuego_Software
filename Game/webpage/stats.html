How stats are tracked
Stats are saved in your browser via localStorage. Each time you visit the Play page and the game loads successfully, a session is recorded. Play time accumulates while the game tab is open and active. Detailed in-game stats (wins, positions, cards used) will be available in a future integration with the game engine.
